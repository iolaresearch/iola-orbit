The gold part us like aluminum or cloth right not solid gold metal or??

No 😭 that gold layer is not solid gold metal.

What you’re seeing is usually one of these:

MLI insulation, Multi-Layer Insulation

Kapton film

Gold-colored thermal blanket material

Sometimes aluminized polyimide

It’s basically thermal protection cloth/foil material wrapped around the satellite.

Purpose:

Protect electronics from extreme temperature swings

Reflect solar radiation

Reduce heat loss

Shield sensitive systems

In orbit temperatures can swing brutally:

Direct sunlight: +120°C+

Shadow/eclipse: -100°C or worse

Without thermal control, components fail fast.

That wrinkled gold look is actually normal and realistic. Real CubeSats and larger satellites often look exactly like that because the insulation blankets are flexible, not rigid armor plating.

For your design specifically:

The frame/body structure underneath is aluminum alloy, usually aerospace-grade aluminum.

The gold outer layer is thermal insulation wrapping.

Solar panels mount on top of the structural frame.

Cameras protrude through cutouts in the blanket.

And honestly, the updated render looks way more believable now. The first render looked like a clean CGI concept. The gold thermal wrapping suddenly makes it feel like an actual flight article/prototype.

def conjunction_risk_score(
    effective_distance,
    relative_velocity,
    time_to_encounter
):

    score = (
        relative_velocity /
        (
            effective_distance *
            (time_to_encounter + 1)
        )
    )

    return score
